# Open Stall

**A cozy symbol-builder roguelite about running a market stall.** Stock a 5-reel stall with produce, crafts and curios; symbols combo, rent escalates, and you engineer absurd synergies until you can buy the shop outright — or go bust.

Free on Google Play with a one-time $4.99 full-game unlock. No ads, no consumables, no accounts, fully offline.
