Open Stall: Market Builder

Stock a market stall, find the combos, pay the fee, buy the shop on the square.

Get it on Google Play

Open Stall: Market Builder is played in the Android app.

Twelve Saturdays. One trestle table. Open Stall is a cozy builder about running a market stall. Buy stock from the wholesaler after each browse: apples, sourdough, honey, tulips, wooden spoons, a brass compass. Lay it out on a fifteen-space stall, item by item, and let the customers browse. What sits next to what decides what they spend: honey next to tea, jam that sells through the fruit beside it, candles on a foggy day, a row of bread. Before they come, the stall shows what your layout will take.

Find the combos. 120 items in six families, each with one rule in plain words. 80 charms that bend the rules. Five weathers, thirty market days with their own events. Every combo you ever trigger goes in the journal, with its rule.

Pay the fee. Stock costs coins, and the stall fee climbs every week on a published schedule. Buy the shop on the square before the twelfth Saturday, or go bust and try a new stock next season.

The Village Green plays in full at no cost, season after season. One in-app purchase opens Harbour Market, where it rains more days than not, City Square, with four browses a day, the endless Free Market after each shop you buy, and the last 36 kinds of stock and 28 charms.

  • Plays fully offline, with no account. Five minutes or forty-five.
  • Portrait, one-handed, large-text and high-contrast options. Nothing hidden behind a hover.
  • A journal of every combo you find, kept between seasons.
