## Full description
**A cozy market sim and roguelite builder: one trestle table, twelve Saturdays, and a shop on the square to save up for.**

**Lay out the stall.** Fifteen spaces on a trestle table. Buy stock from the wholesaler after each browse (apples, sourdough, honey, tulips, wooden spoons, a brass compass) and set it out item by item. Before the customers come, the stall shows what your layout will take.

**Find the combos.** What sits next to what decides what they spend: honey beside the tea, jam that sells through the fruit next to it, candles on a foggy day, a row of bread. 120 kinds of stock in six families, each with one rule in plain words, and 80 charms that bend the rules. Every combo you trigger goes in the journal, with its rule.

**Read the day.** Five kinds of weather and thirty market-day events, from a school trip that makes sweet things pay more to a food critic who doubles the first browse. Plan the layout around them.

**Pay the fee.** The stall fee climbs every Saturday. Buy the shop on the square before the twelfth Saturday, or go bust and start a new season with what you learned. Reach week six and new stock and charms join the pool.

**The Village Green plays in full at no cost**, season after season, with 84 kinds of stock and 52 charms. One in-app purchase unlocks Harbour Market, where the quayside row pays double on a dry day and half in the rain; City Square, with four browses a day and a customer's order every morning; the endless Free Market after each shop you buy; and the last 36 kinds of stock and 28 charms.

- A strategy puzzle in every layout, with a journal of every combo you find, kept between seasons
- Offline game: no account, no internet needed, no ads
- Portrait play with large-text, high-contrast and reduce-motion options
